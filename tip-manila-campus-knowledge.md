## Gates and streets
- Casal Campus: Gate 1 (P. Casal St. side), Gate 2, Gate 3. Streets: P. Casal St., Arlegui St.
- Arlegui Campus: one gate facing Arlegui St.

## Ambiguities (ask the user)
- OSA appears in F-1xx (Casal) and A-2xx (Arlegui)
- OVPAA appears in A-2xx (Arlegui) and PC-12-2xx (Casal)
- Chapel appears in PC-12-1xx (Casal, next to PC 12) and A-2xx (Arlegui)
- Canteen, Study Area and Clinic exist at Casal Campus (open areas/facilities on the map) and on A-1xx (Arlegui)
- Seminar Room appears in PC-5-1xx and PC-12-1xx

## Limits
- The map gives floor-level groupings (e.g., F-3xx), not individual room numbers.
- Acronyms (OSA, SRO, OVPSAS, OVPAA, QMO, CES, CEA, NSTP) are not expanded on the map; do not guess meanings.
- Floor 3 of F is listed only as Classrooms; specific classroom numbers are unknown.
